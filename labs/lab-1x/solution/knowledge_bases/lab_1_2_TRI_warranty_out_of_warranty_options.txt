Focus Corp — Out-of-Warranty Resolution Options

When a product is confirmed to be outside the warranty period, the specialist presents an offer based on the customer's loyalty tier:

- Standard customers: no automatic entitlement; specialist may escalate to supervisor.
- Gold customers: 10% discount code for a replacement purchase in the Focus Corp online shop.
- Platinum customers: goodwill replacement offer or 20% discount code for a replacement purchase in the Focus Corp online shop.

The appropriate offer is presented based on the customer's loyalty tier, which is available in the customer profile.
