Technical Troubleshooting Guide

Product Category: Smart Watch

Smart Watch Not Syncing

Product: Smart Watch Ultra

Symptoms:
- Data not updating
- Watch not connecting to phone
- App shows disconnected
- Notifications not received

Troubleshooting Steps:

Step 1: Check App Permissions
- Ensure app has Bluetooth permission
- Enable background app refresh
- Allow notifications
- Expected outcome: Permissions granted

Step 2: Verify Connection
- Open companion app
- Check connection status
- Ensure watch is within range (30 feet)
- Expected outcome: Connected status shown

Step 3: Force Sync
- Pull down to refresh in app
- Restart watch
- Restart phone
- Re-pair devices if needed
- Expected outcome: Data syncs

Step 4: Data Not Updating
- Check internet connection
- Verify account login
- Clear app cache
- Update app to latest version

- Expected outcome: Data updates
