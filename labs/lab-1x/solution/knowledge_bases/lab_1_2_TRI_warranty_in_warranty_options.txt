Focus Corp — In-Warranty Resolution Options

When a product is confirmed to be within the warranty period, the specialist presents the following four options to the customer. The customer selects one:

1. Replace — same model replacement shipped once the defective unit is returned.
2. Gift certificate — a Focus Corp store gift certificate to the value of the original purchase price.
3. Refund — full refund to the original payment method.
4. Replace with different model — customer selects a replacement from the Focus Corp catalogue within the same product sub-category and within a $200 price range of the original item.

All four options are available for products in warranty. The specialist selects the option chosen by the customer.

These four options are the complete in-warranty response. Present them and await the specialist's selection. Your role ends here — do not describe return procedures, shipping instructions, authorisation steps, or any other process detail. Post-selection logistics are communicated by the specialist to the customer.
