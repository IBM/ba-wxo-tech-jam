Technical Troubleshooting Guide

Product Category: Headphones

Device Won't Turn On

Product: Wireless Headphones Pro

Symptoms:
- Device does not power on
- No LED indicators
- No response to power button

Troubleshooting Steps:

Step 1: Check Power Source
- Try different outlet
- Verify outlet is working with another device
- Check for tripped circuit breaker
- Expected outcome: Device should show charging indicator

Step 2: Inspect Charging Cable
- Look for visible damage to cable
- Try different USB cable if available
- Check for debris in charging port
- Clean charging port gently with dry cloth
- Expected outcome: Proper connection established

Step 3: Perform Hard Reset
- Press and hold power button for 10 seconds
- Release and wait 5 seconds
- Press power button normally to turn on
- Expected outcome: Device powers on

Step 4: Check LED Indicators
- Red light: Charging in progress
- Green light: Fully charged
- No light: Possible hardware issue
- Flashing red: Battery critically low

Step 5: Battery Deep Discharge Recovery
- Leave device on charger for 2 hours minimum
- Do not attempt to turn on during charging
- Try turning on after 2 hours
- Expected outcome: Battery recovers and device powers on


Bluetooth Connection Issues

Product: Wireless Headphones Pro

Symptoms:
- Cannot find device in Bluetooth list
- Connection drops frequently
- Audio quality issues
- Pairing fails

Troubleshooting Steps:

Step 1: Basic Connection
- Turn on Bluetooth on phone/device
- Put headphones in pairing mode (hold power 5 seconds)
- Look for device in available devices list
- Select headphones to pair
- Expected outcome: Successful pairing

Step 2: Connection Drops
- Move closer to device (within 30 feet)
- Remove obstacles between devices
- Turn off other Bluetooth devices nearby
- Restart both devices
- Expected outcome: Stable connection

Step 3: Can't Find Headphones
- Ensure headphones are charged
- Reset headphones (hold power + volume up for 10 seconds)
- Clear Bluetooth cache on phone
- Forget device and re-pair
- Expected outcome: Device appears in list

Step 4: Audio Quality Issues
- Check for interference from WiFi routers
- Update device firmware
- Adjust audio codec settings
- Clean headphone speakers

- Expected outcome: Clear audio
