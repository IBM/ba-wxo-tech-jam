Technical Troubleshooting Guide

Product Category: Laptop

USB-C Charging Issues

Applies To: Laptops

Symptoms:
- Laptop does not charge through USB-C
- Charging indicator does not appear
- Battery percentage does not increase while connected
- Laptop only charges intermittently through USB-C
- USB-C charger is connected but power is not detected

Troubleshooting Steps:

Step 1: Verify Power Adapter Compatibility
- Confirm the charger supports laptop charging over USB-C
- The Focus Gamer Z2 requires a USB-C power adapter rated at 150 W (see Focus Power supply 150W, catalog item CI-P23SDW)
- If available, test with the manufacturer-approved 150 W USB-C power adapter
- Expected outcome: Compatible charger provides stable power

Step 2: Inspect USB-C Cable and Port
- Check the USB-C cable for visible damage or loose connectors
- Try a different USB-C cable that supports power delivery
- Inspect the laptop USB-C port for dust or debris
- Clean the port gently with dry compressed air or a soft dry tool
- Expected outcome: Proper power connection is established

Step 3: Check Power Source
- Try a different wall outlet
- Verify the outlet works with another device
- Remove any intermediate dock or adapter and connect the charger directly
- Expected outcome: Charger receives stable input power

Step 4: Confirm Charging State
- Leave the laptop connected for at least 15 minutes
- Check whether the charging LED or battery icon changes
- Restart the laptop while connected to power
- Expected outcome: Charging state is detected after reconnect or restart

Step 5: Reduce Power Demand
- Disconnect non-essential USB peripherals
- Close high-power applications
- If the battery is critically low, leave the laptop closed and charging for 30 minutes
- Expected outcome: Laptop begins charging when power demand is reduced

End of Troubleshooting Steps.
These five steps are the complete troubleshooting procedure for USB-C charging issues. No additional steps are needed. If the issue persists after all five steps, escalate to a warranty or hardware repair request.
