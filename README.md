## watsonx Orchestrate (wxO) Tech Jam Series - 3Q2026

We are pleased to announce our upcoming IBM watsonx Orchestrate (wxO) Tech Jam series. The series will consist of three events providing global coverage. All events are free and **open to business partners and IBMers**.

**Event Dates & Registration Links:**
- EMEA, October 14 – 15 –> https://ibm.biz/wxo-tech-jam-emea
- APAC, October 21 – 22 –> https://ibm.biz/wxo-tech-jam-apac
- Americas, October 28 – 29 –> https://ibm.biz/wxo-tech-jam-americas

**Events Location:** Virtual

**Events Description:**

The IBM watsonx Orchestrate (wxO) Tech Jam is a new, hands-on technical learning experience delivered as a 2-day virtual, instructor-led event. Designed as an interactive pilot program, the Tech Jam combines guided labs, expert presentations, live demonstrations, and opportunities for direct engagement with product specialists.

Throughout the event, subject matter experts will share their knowledge, vision, roadmaps, and practical experience in agentic AI, automation, and enterprise orchestration.

Attendees will experience the full value of IBM watsonx Orchestrate through an immersive, hands-on learning model. Participants will learn how to design and build modern multi-agent systems, leverage tools and knowledge bases, orchestrate agentic workflows, and apply AI to solve realistic business challenges and drive measurable outcomes.

The breadth and depth of IBM watsonx Orchestrate capabilities are showcased through Trinity, a realistic, end-to-end AI-powered Customer Support Specialist scenario that reflects real-world enterprise challenges and opportunities.

Built on the principles of agentic AI, Trinity serves as the foundation for a series of guided labs and business scenarios that demonstrate how intelligent agents can collaborate, reason, and take action across systems and processes.

Participants can choose to explore the complete solution journey from start to finish, providing a comprehensive understanding of the platform, or dive deeper into one of many prepared development-focused labs — or combine both approaches for a customized learning experience.

In addition to the hands-on exercises, the labs are infused with practical best practices, architectural guidance, design patterns, and reasoning strategies to help participants better understand how to successfully design, build, and deploy enterprise-grade agentic solutions.

Beyond learning, exploring, and networking with subject matter experts, participants will have the opportunity to provide direct feedback to IBM product management and development teams, helping shape the future direction of the platform.
